Weekly Early Warning System

1. Objective

Develop a weekly monitoring approach to identify declining delivery performance early enough for management to investigate and take corrective action.

2. Key Performance Indicators

- On-time delivery percentage
- Average route delay
- Vehicle breakdown rate
- Average warehouse delay
- Total weekly shipments

3. Proposed Warning Rules

The system will monitor weekly operational performance.

A warning should be generated when:

1. On-time delivery falls below its recent four-week average by more than 3 percentage points.
2. Route delays increase significantly.
3. Vehicle breakdown rates increase.
4. Warehouse processing delays increase.

4. Warning Levels

- Normal: Performance is stable.
- Warning: One or more KPIs show deterioration.
- Critical: Multiple KPIs deteriorate for consecutive weeks.

5. Management Actions

- Review delayed routes.
- Inspect vehicles with repeated breakdowns.
- Investigate warehouse loading delays.
- Assign corrective actions to the responsible teams.
- Review progress in the next weekly meeting.

6. Validation

The proposed warning system must be tested using correctly timestamped historical shipment records.

To demonstrate detection at least four weeks before a major decline, backtesting must show that the warning was generated at least four weeks before the decline occurred.

7. Limitation

The current project uses synthetic demonstration data. It cannot establish that a real operational decline was predicted four weeks in advance.

8. Conclusion

A weekly KPI monitoring system can support earlier investigation of logistics performance problems. Real company data and historical backtesting are necessary before deployment.
