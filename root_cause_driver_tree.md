
Root Cause Driver Tree – Kolkata Logistics

1. Problem Statement

The demonstration analysis shows that on-time delivery performance declined between Q1 and Q2.

KPI| Q1| Q2
On-time delivery| 89.01%| 78.83%
Warehouse delay| 2.40 hours| 2.99 hours

The decline is approximately 10.18 percentage points.

4. Recommended Actions

1. Identify routes with frequent delays.
2. Review vehicle maintenance records.
3. Improve warehouse loading and dispatch processes.
4. Monitor hub-wise delivery performance.
5. Investigate delayed shipments every week.

5. Driver Contribution Analysis

The combined contribution of operational factors must be calculated using real shipment data and a validated statistical model.

The target is to explain at least 80% of the observed decline.

This target has not yet been demonstrated by the synthetic dataset.

6. Conclusion

Route delays, vehicle breakdowns and warehouse delays are priority areas for further investigation.

Actual company records are required to confirm root causes and quantify their contribution.

Data limitation: This report uses synthetic demonstration data, not actual Kolkata company records.
