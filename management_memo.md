Management Memo: Logistics Root Cause Analysis

Project: Kolkata Freight Trucking Company
Analysis: Quarterly Delivery Performance
Data status: Synthetic demonstration data, not actual company records.

1. Executive Summary

The demonstration analysis shows a decline in on-time delivery performance between Q1 and Q2.

- Q1 on-time delivery: 89.0%
- Q2 on-time delivery: 78.8%
- Decline: approximately 10.2 percentage points.
- Average warehouse delay increased from 2.40 hours to 2.99 hours.

These results demonstrate how operational data can be used to investigate delivery performance.

2. Key Findings

Statistical tests identified significant differences in:

1. Route delays between quarters.
2. Vehicle breakdown and on-time delivery.
3. Warehouse delays between quarters.

The tests did not identify statistically significant differences in hub performance, service type or festive-season groups.

3. Recommended Actions

- Monitor route delays and investigate frequently delayed routes.
- Improve vehicle maintenance and inspection schedules.
- Review warehouse loading and dispatch processes.
- Track weekly on-time delivery performance.
- Investigate operational changes before implementing corrective actions.

4. Early Warning System

A weekly monitoring system is proposed to identify falling delivery performance.

The system should alert management when on-time delivery falls below its recent baseline. It should be validated using correctly ordered, real shipment records before operational use.

5. Limitations

This project uses synthetic demonstration data. The findings do not establish actual causes of a company's performance decline.

Real shipment, route, vehicle, warehouse and scan-event data are required to validate the hypotheses, quantify the drivers and establish whether they explain at least 80% of the decline.

6. Conclusion

The demonstration identifies route delays, vehicle breakdowns and warehouse delays as areas worth investigating. Further analysis using real company data is necessary before making final business decisions.
